# Dashboard Spec

- **Executive Marketing Overview**: Are marketing dollars producing efficient revenue and margin? Filters: Reporting Month, Channel Group, Region, Owner Team.
- **Channel Performance**: Which channels are scaling efficiently and which need budget reallocation? Filters: Reporting Month, Channel Group, Device, Region.
- **Campaign Intelligence**: Which campaigns are wasting budget or driving high-value customers? Filters: Campaign, Channel, Owner Team, Waste Budget Flag.
- **Funnel Analysis**: Where are leads dropping before revenue conversion? Filters: Reporting Month, Channel, Region, Sales Rep.
- **Attribution & ROI**: Why do attribution reports disagree across systems? Filters: Reporting Month, Campaign, Channel, Attribution Model.
- **Target vs Actual**: Are teams meeting regional budget, lead, and revenue targets? Filters: Target Month, Region, Channel, Budget Owner.
- **Governance & Action Center**: Can leaders trust the dashboard, and who owns the next fixes? Filters: Owner Team, Score Status, Priority, Action Type, Certified Status.
- **GA4 Funnel**: Where does the GA4-style journey drop before purchase? Filters: Event Date, Source, Medium, Campaign, Device.
- **Variance Drivers**: Which diagnostic movements contributed to ROAS and CAC change? Filters: Reporting Month, Channel, Severity, Primary Driver.
- **Campaign Action Center**: Which transparent actions should marketing leadership prioritize? Filters: Reporting Month, Channel, Priority, Recommended Action, Data Quality Status.
- **Scenario Planning**: How do explicit budget, CPC, conversion, AOV, and growth assumptions change planning outcomes? Filters: Scenario Name, Channel, Simulation Status.
