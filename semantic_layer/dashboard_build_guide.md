# Dashboard Build Guide

## 1. Executive Marketing Overview

Business question: Are we growing efficiently across spend, revenue, and conversion outcomes?

Metrics:

- Spend
- Booked Revenue
- Gross Margin
- ROAS
- MER
- CAC
- Revenue Attainment
- Budget Recommendation

Filters and drilldowns:

- Month
- Channel group
- Region
- Product family
- Campaign owner

## 2. Channel Performance

Business question: Which channels produce the best return and conversion efficiency?

Metrics:

- Spend
- Impressions
- Clicks
- CTR
- CPC
- Leads
- Qualified Leads
- CAC
- ROAS

Filters and drilldowns:

- Channel
- Region
- Device
- Month
- Paid vs owned channel

## 3. Campaign Intelligence

Business question: Which campaigns should be scaled, fixed, or paused?

Metrics:

- Spend
- Attributed Revenue
- Attributed ROAS
- Platform Conversions
- Waste Budget Flag
- CPC
- CTR

Filters and drilldowns:

- Campaign
- Channel
- Owner team
- Source system
- Region

## 4. Funnel Analysis

Business question: Where do leads drop off between acquisition and sales conversion?

Metrics:

- Total Leads
- MQLs
- Sales Accepted Leads
- SQLs
- Closed Won Conversions
- Lead to MQL Rate
- MQL to SQL Rate
- SQL to Close Rate
- Average Conversion Lag

Filters and drilldowns:

- Channel
- Sales rep team
- Region
- Lead source
- Month

## 5. Attribution and ROI

Business question: How does channel ROI change under different attribution models?

Metrics:

- Attributed Revenue
- Weighted Conversions
- First Touch Revenue
- Last Touch Revenue
- Linear Revenue
- Average Touchpoint Weight

Filters and drilldowns:

- Attribution model
- Campaign
- Channel
- Conversion month
- Product

## 6. Target vs Actual

Business question: Are regions and channels on pace against spend, lead, conversion, and revenue goals?

Metrics:

- Target Spend
- Actual Spend
- Spend Attainment
- Target Revenue
- Actual Revenue
- Revenue Attainment
- Lead Attainment
- Conversion Attainment

Filters and drilldowns:

- Month
- Region
- Channel
- Budget owner

## 7. Data Quality and Monitoring

Business question: Can stakeholders trust that today's data is fresh, complete, and valid?

Metrics:

- Last Ingestion Time
- Failed Loads
- Source Rows
- Rejected Rows
- Validation Failures
- Monitoring Status

Filters and drilldowns:

- Source system
- Rule name
- Severity
- Batch
- Date
