# Page Specs

1. Executive Marketing Overview: spend, revenue, ROAS, CAC, target status, source-health status.
2. Channel Performance: channel spend, leads, conversions, ROAS, CAC, trend and rank visuals.
3. Campaign ROI Deep Dive: campaign table, ROI flags, action recommendations, drill-through detail.
4. Funnel Conversion: lead, MQL, SQL, and close-rate drop-off visuals.
5. Budget Pacing & Targets: spend/revenue attainment, pacing status, variance table.
6. Attribution & Customer Value: attribution model comparison and customer segment value.
7. Data Quality & Refresh Health: rejected rows, source health, validation warnings, refresh notes.
8. GA4 Funnel: session and ecommerce-stage drop-off by source, campaign and device.
9. Performance Drivers and Action Center: diagnostic variance plus human-reviewed actions.
10. Scenario Planning: explicit assumptions, projected outcomes and target variance.
