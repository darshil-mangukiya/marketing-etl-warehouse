# Business Insights and Recommendations

These example findings come from generated project outputs and show how the marts support business review. They do not represent company performance.

| Finding | Evidence/Mart | Root Cause Hypothesis | Business Impact | Recommendation | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok can show lower acquisition cost but weaker closed-won quality. | `mart_channel_performance`, `mart_funnel_performance` | TikTok campaigns may drive inexpensive top-of-funnel engagement without enough qualified intent. | Low CAC may overstate value if SQL and close rates lag. | Compare CAC with SQL-to-close rate before scaling spend. | Growth Marketing Manager |
| Google Ads can have higher CPC but stronger revenue per conversion. | `mart_channel_performance`, `mart_campaign_performance` | Search intent can produce fewer but higher-value conversions. | Budget cuts based only on CPC could reduce revenue quality. | Review ROAS and margin before reducing paid search budget. | CMO / VP Marketing |
| Facebook can drive lead volume with lower SQL conversion. | `mart_funnel_performance` | Targeting or lead forms may capture low-intent leads. | Sales teams may spend time on lower-quality leads. | Review campaign audience and lead qualification criteria. | Sales Operations Manager |
| Late-arriving conversions can change attribution after initial reporting. | `docs/incremental_load_evidence.md`, `sales_conversions` | Sales close dates and `updated_at` values arrive after original touchpoints. | ROI reports can shift after the first dashboard refresh. | Use watermark refresh and attribution reconciliation before monthly signoff. | Analytics Engineer |
| Missing attribution IDs create ROI gaps. | `mart_journey_quality`, `mart_data_quality_monitoring` | UTM or platform identifiers are not consistently captured. | Campaign performance can be under-attributed or misallocated. | Add source-level checks and flag campaigns with low attribution coverage. | Data Engineer |
| Mobile sessions can convert at the session level but drop during lead qualification. | `mart_device_performance`, `mart_funnel_performance` | Mobile form quality or follow-up process may be weaker. | Lead volume may not translate to pipeline. | Review mobile form friction and qualification rules. | Growth Marketing Manager |
| Regional target gaps affect budget planning. | `mart_target_vs_actual`, `mart_budget_pacing` | Spend and target allocations may not match regional demand. | Finance and marketing may misread under/over-performance. | Review variance by region/channel during monthly planning. | Finance Manager |
| Campaigns with unmapped IDs create data quality risk. | `dim_campaign`, `mart_data_quality_monitoring` | Source naming conventions may not be aligned with reference mappings. | Dashboards may group spend under unknown or incorrect campaigns. | Resolve unmapped campaigns before executive reporting. | BI Developer |
| Budget pacing can identify overspend before month-end. | `mart_budget_pacing` | Spend can exceed target before revenue catches up. | Teams can correct allocation before the reporting period closes. | Add pacing review to weekly performance meeting. | Finance Manager |
| Source freshness failures should block executive reporting review. | `mart_source_health`, observability dashboard | One or more source loads may be stale or incomplete. | Leadership could make decisions from incomplete data. | Require source-health check before publishing executive summaries. | Data Engineer |
