# P2 Recruiter Brief

## Project in 20 seconds

I built a governed campaign ROI and marketing decision-intelligence platform, then used it as an analyst: six modeled business requests move from clarification and data validation to SQL/Python analysis, executive storytelling, and human-reviewed recommendations.

## Project in 60 seconds

The project integrates generated paid-media, website, CRM, conversion, target, and reference data into governed dbt marts across local DuckDB, PostgreSQL, and a bounded BigQuery/GA4 path. Airflow orchestrates the workflow; Power BI and Streamlit expose ROI, funnel, attribution, target, customer-value, and quality views. The P2 2.0 layer demonstrates analyst execution: six modeled requests, reproducible results and charts, a 10-slide management review, a concise executive brief, and a release workflow that can place recommendations on DATA QUALITY HOLD.

## Three strongest accomplishments

1. **Decision-ready analysis:** six high-quality ad-hoc investigations with clarification, grain, validation, evidence, interpretation, recommendation, caveat, and concise response.
2. **Executive communication:** a reproducible 10-slide PowerPoint that converts performance, funnel, attribution, target, scenario, and trust signals into a management narrative.
3. **Governed analytics depth:** 44 enabled local dbt models plus 53 data tests, a 28-task Airflow DAG, six attribution methods, Power BI/Streamlit reporting, and source-to-recommendation quality controls.

## Exact evidence boundaries

- Marketing business data and performance results are generated/synthetic.
- The bounded real portfolio-site GA4 Daily BigQuery export is separate from generated marketing inputs.
- No real customer, ad-spend, campaign-performance, realized-impact, stakeholder-adoption, approval, or sign-off claim is made.
- Attribution is descriptive allocation, not causal lift.
- Budget scenarios are deterministic SIMULATED / WHAT-IF outputs, not optimal allocations or forecasts.
- No live ad-account execution, Power BI Service deployment, production operation, enterprise SLA, or production-scale 38M-row claim is made.
