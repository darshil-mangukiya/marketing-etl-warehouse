# Dashboard Requirements


| Page | Business question | Required filters |
|---|---|---|
| Executive Marketing Overview | Are spend, revenue, ROAS, CAC, targets, and source health acceptable? | date, channel, region |
| Channel Performance | Which channels produce the strongest efficiency? | date, channel |
| Campaign ROI Deep Dive | Which campaigns should be scaled, paused, or reviewed? | date, channel, campaign |
| Funnel Conversion | Where is the largest funnel drop-off? | date, channel, region |
| Budget Pacing and Targets | Where are budgets or targets off plan? | month, region, channel |
| Attribution and Customer Value | How does model choice change ROI and value interpretation? | model, channel, segment |
| Data Quality and Refresh Health | Which sources have reporting risk? | source, status, date |


Dashboard requirements are validated with generated source profiles and local BI artifacts; no customer, employer, or ad-platform records are included.
