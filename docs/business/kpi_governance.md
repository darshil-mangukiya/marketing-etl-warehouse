# Kpi Governance


KPI governance uses `semantic/kpi_catalog.yml` as the machine-readable source for definitions, formulas, grain, inputs, and categories. Any report measure should trace back to the catalog or to the generated DAX measure catalog.

Governed KPIs include ROAS, campaign ROI, CAC, cost per lead, lead-to-MQL rate, SQL-to-close rate, spend attainment, revenue attainment, attribution coverage, and data product score.


KPI examples use generated marketing and revenue profiles, keeping the governance workflow reviewable without customer, employer, or ad-platform records.
