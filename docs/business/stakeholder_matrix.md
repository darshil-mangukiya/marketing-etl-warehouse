# Stakeholder Matrix


| Stakeholder | Primary decisions | Needs from the platform |
|---|---|---|
| Marketing leadership | Budget allocation, channel mix, campaign strategy | ROAS, CAC, target attainment, executive insights |
| Campaign managers | Scale, pause, monitor, optimize | campaign ROI, action recommendations, funnel quality |
| Finance | Budget pacing and variance review | spend attainment, revenue attainment, target variance |
| Sales operations | Lead handoff and close-rate review | funnel conversion, SQL-to-close, lead quality signals |
| Executive leadership | High-level performance and risk | concise scorecards, risks, caveats, next actions |
| BI team | Report build and semantic governance | star schema, DAX catalog, relationships, page specs |
| Data engineering team | Reliability and source health | logs, tests, data-quality outputs, status files |


Stakeholder responsibilities are mapped to generated pipeline, warehouse, and BI artifacts used for local review.
