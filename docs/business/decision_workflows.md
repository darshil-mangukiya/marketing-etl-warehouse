# Decision Workflows


## Scale

Scale when ROAS is strong, conversion quality is healthy, and budget pacing is not already excessive.

## Pause

Pause or reduce spend when a campaign has high spend, weak ROAS, and weak conversion rate.

## Budget Reallocation

Reallocate when target attainment is low and another campaign or channel shows better efficiency.

## Funnel Investigation

Investigate when clicks or leads exist but MQL, SQL, or closed-won rates are weak.

## Attribution Investigation

Investigate when conversions exist but attributed revenue coverage is low or model deltas change interpretation.

## Source-Health Investigation

Investigate source health when rejected rows, freshness failures, mapping gaps, or validation warnings are visible.


Decision workflows are modeled with generated source profiles so funnel, ROI, budget, and quality review paths can be evaluated without real business records.
