# Reporting Workflow Current Vs Future


Current state: teams export platform data manually, reconcile it in spreadsheets, calculate inconsistent KPIs, and rebuild broken dashboards when schemas or naming conventions change.

Future state: the platform generates governed marts, quality outputs, KPI definitions, analyst recommendations, executive insights, and Power BI-ready handoff assets so stakeholders can review the same trusted reporting layer.


The workflow uses generated source profiles so current-state and future-state reporting can be reviewed without real customer or ad-platform data.
