# Business Problem


Manual campaign reporting combines ad, web, CRM, sales, target, and quality data in spreadsheets. The business cannot consistently answer which channels produce the strongest ROAS, which campaigns should be scaled or paused, where the funnel leaks, why attribution models disagree, and whether dashboards are safe for stakeholder reporting.

This project models the future-state reporting workflow: generated marts, governed KPI logic, source-health checks, analyst outputs, Power BI-ready handoff assets, and executive insight summaries.


The business scenario uses generated marketing, CRM, sales, and target profiles so the reporting problem can be reviewed safely without real company data.
