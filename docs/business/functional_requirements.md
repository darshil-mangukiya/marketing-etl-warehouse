# Functional Requirements


1. Generate or ingest simulated paid media, web, CRM, sales, target, and mapping data.
2. Validate source records and produce source-health evidence.
3. Build reporting marts for channel performance, campaign ROI, funnel conversion, targets, attribution, customer value, and data quality.
4. Generate analyst CSV outputs for ROI drivers, recommendations, executive insights, and alerts.
5. Provide Power BI-ready import tables, DAX measures, relationship guidance, and page specifications.
6. Provide role-based documentation for Data Analyst, Business Analyst, and BI Developer review.


Functional requirements are exercised with generated source profiles, local pipeline outputs, and BI artifacts rather than real customer or ad-platform data.
