# Business Requirements Document

## 1. Business Context

A modeled SaaS and ecommerce business provides the requirements context for paid media, website, CRM, sales, targets, and reference systems. Teams need a trusted view of marketing spend, funnel quality, attribution, revenue contribution, budget pacing, and data quality. Local source records are generated by the project pipeline.

## 2. Problem Statement

Marketing reporting depends on fragmented exports from Google Ads, Facebook Ads, TikTok Ads, website analytics, CRM leads, sales conversions, targets, and campaign mappings. Manual spreadsheet reporting creates inconsistent campaign names, missing attribution IDs, late conversion handling gaps, and unreliable dashboard trust.

## 3. Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Primary Need |
|---|---|
| CMO / VP Marketing | Channel ROI, campaign profitability, budget reallocation, target progress |
| Growth Marketing Manager | Campaign optimization, waste detection, attribution completeness |
| Sales Operations Manager | Lead quality, stage conversion, sales handoff visibility |
| Finance Manager | Budget pacing, target vs actual, revenue and margin tracking |
| BI Developer | Governed KPI definitions, semantic relationships, dashboard validation |
| Data Engineer | Reliable ingestion, validation, logging, watermarks, monitoring |
| Analytics Engineer | dbt models, conformed dimensions, reusable marts, lineage |
| Executive Leadership | Trusted KPI overview and exception-based decision support |

## 4. Pain Points

- Campaign names and IDs differ across source systems.
- Attribution identifiers are missing or inconsistent.
- Late-arriving sales conversions change ROI after initial reporting.
- Manual exports create slow, fragile spreadsheet workflows.
- Dashboards can break when source schemas drift.
- Data quality issues are not visible before business review.
- KPI formulas are not centrally governed.

## 5. Business Objectives

- Provide trusted channel, campaign, funnel, attribution, and target reporting.
- Reduce manual spreadsheet dependency by publishing reusable marts and exports.
- Flag missing attribution, rejected records, stale sources, and mapping issues.
- Support budget review, campaign optimization, funnel quality review, and executive reporting.
- Maintain clear semantic definitions for reporting metrics.

## 6. Scope

- Source generation for paid media, website analytics, CRM leads, sales conversions, targets, and mappings.
- API-style and file-based ingestion into a local S3-style lake.
- Validation, rejected records, source audit metadata, watermarks, and processed-file tracking.
- PostgreSQL raw/ops loading and dbt warehouse/mart modeling.
- BI-ready CSV marts, KPI catalog, DAX catalog, relationship guidance, Streamlit review app, and generated reports.

## 7. Deployment Extensions

- Managed vendor credentials, OAuth hardening, and production API quota administration.
- Cloud deployment, managed refresh, gateway configuration, and workspace administration.
- Enterprise data access, certified datasets, and governed production data sharing.
- Causal incrementality, marketing mix modeling, and streaming extensions.

## 8. Assumptions

- Local outputs use records generated from execution profiles.
- Smoke mode is the default validation mode for local development.
- The configured scale profile documents larger volume and is tied to run manifests when executed.
- BI users consume curated marts rather than raw source tables.

## 9. Constraints

- The platform is local-first and Dockerized.
- Power BI assets include an editable Desktop `.pbix`, screenshot evidence, source-controlled semantic docs, and setup guidance.
- Some source-quality failures are intentionally generated to demonstrate validation behavior.
- Local runtime must remain lightweight enough for repeatable validation.

## 10. Success Criteria

- Source data is ingested with row counts, audit records, and rejected-record outputs.
- Watermarks and processed-file hashes support incremental reruns.
- dbt models create staging, intermediate, core, and reporting layers.
- KPI definitions are documented and tied to marts.
- Dashboard-ready exports reconcile to mart totals.
- Data freshness and validation status are visible before reporting review.
- Local quality gate passes or records documented local-environment skips.

## 11. Reporting Requirements

- Executive overview for spend, revenue, ROAS, CAC, margin, target attainment, and source health.
- Channel performance by date, channel, region, and device.
- Campaign intelligence for spend efficiency, conversion performance, and budget waste.
- Funnel analysis from sessions and leads to qualified stages and conversions.
- Attribution and ROI comparisons across first-touch, last-touch, and weighted models.
- Target vs actual and budget pacing by month, region, and channel.
- Data quality monitoring by source, rule, rejected rows, and freshness status.

## 12. Data Requirements

| Data Domain | Required Elements |
|---|---|
| Paid media | spend, impressions, clicks, conversions, campaign IDs, attribution IDs |
| Website analytics | sessions, device, geography, source, UTM campaign, attribution ID |
| CRM leads | lead ID, source, stage, assigned rep, CDC operation, updated timestamp |
| Sales conversions | conversion ID, lead/customer linkage, deal value, product, close date, updated timestamp |
| Targets | monthly spend, revenue, lead, and conversion targets by region/channel |
| Mappings | campaign, region, channel, device, source-system normalization |

## 13. Functional Requirements

- Generate repeatable source batches by execution profile.
- Ingest API-style and file-based inputs.
- Validate required schemas, business rules, and source contracts.
- Separate accepted and rejected records.
- Maintain ingestion metadata, audit logs, watermarks, and processed-file state.
- Load raw data into PostgreSQL.
- Build dbt staging, intermediate, core warehouse, and reporting marts.
- Export BI-ready analysis datasets.
- Produce monitoring, governance, and reporting outputs.

## 14. Non-Functional Requirements

- Local reproducibility through Python and Docker Compose.
- Idempotent reruns for repeated local validation.
- Clear documentation for setup, data sources, model lineage, and project scope.
- Modular code organization by generation, ingestion, modeling, monitoring, reporting, and semantic layers.
- Automated validation through tests and generated artifacts.

## 15. KPI Requirements

- Spend, booked revenue, gross margin, ROAS, MER, CAC.
- CTR, CPC, clicks, impressions, leads, qualified leads, closed-won conversions.
- Lead-to-MQL, MQL-to-SQL, and SQL-to-close rates.
- Target attainment, spend attainment, revenue attainment, lead attainment.
- Attributed revenue, weighted conversions, attribution coverage.
- Rejected rows, source health, freshness, quality failures.

## 16. Acceptance Criteria

- KPI formulas match `semantic_layer/kpi_catalog.md` and `semantic_layer/dax_measure_catalog.md`.
- Dashboard exports support filters by date, channel, region, device, campaign, and attribution model where relevant.
- Missing attribution IDs and campaign mapping gaps are visible in quality or journey outputs.
- Export totals reconcile to the corresponding mart files.
- Freshness/source-health status is visible in monitoring outputs.
- Rejected rows and quality failures are documented with source and rule context.

## 17. Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Generated project results are mistaken for company performance | Identify data provenance in project and BI documentation |
| Dashboard totals drift from marts | Validate DAX and dashboard totals against exported marts |
| Late conversions alter historical ROI | Use `updated_at` watermarks and refresh affected marts |
| Missing attribution IDs reduce ROI confidence | Flag missing attribution and expose coverage metrics |
| Source schema drift breaks ingestion | Use source contracts and validation reports |
| Manual Power BI setup introduces relationship errors | Provide relationship map, setup guide, and UAT checklist |

## 18. Modeled marketing leadership questions for the cloud upgrade

These modeled questions define the decision needs covered by the reporting requirements.

1. Which channels generate the strongest governed ROAS?
2. Which campaigns are candidates for controlled scaling?
3. Which campaigns should be reduced, repaired, or placed on a data-quality hold?
4. Which diagnostic drivers contributed to the latest ROAS and CAC change?
5. At which GA4-style event or CRM stage does the funnel drop most sharply?
6. Which campaigns miss spend, revenue, lead, conversion, ROAS, or CAC targets?
7. Why do attribution models disagree, and do their allocated totals reconcile?
8. Which sources are stale, failed, rejected, or missing a watermark?
9. Which actions should marketing leadership prioritize, and which metric should be monitored?
10. Can each reported KPI be traced to source, transformation, test, visual, and acceptance criterion?
